We cut for retention, not for showreels.

The standard

A clip that looks good in isolation is worth nothing if people leave before it lands. Every decision Cutveo makes — pacing, cut points, what gets thrown away — is made against the retention graph.

How we work

One editor on your channel, not a rotating pool. You send footage, you get a cut back with a note on what was changed and why. Revisions are part of the process, not a surcharge.

(02) — Bio

Who’s behind it

Cutveo is run by its founder — a full-time video editor and channel manager who spent years cutting other people’s content before putting a name on the work.

That work was not freelance-gig editing. It meant owning channels end to end: deciding what got made, cutting it, packaging it, and watching what the numbers said afterwards. The habit that came out of it is simple — the edit answers to the audience, not to the editor’s taste.

Cutveo exists because that skill set is now available to brands and creators directly, without an agency layer sitting between the client and the person actually cutting the video.
